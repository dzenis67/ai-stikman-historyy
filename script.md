# Napoleon's Real Achievement: Script

**Format:** vertical 9:16, about 30 seconds, one calm narrator
**Length:** 76 words

---

## 1. Narration (word for word)

> This is Napoleon's hat.
> Most people remember him for his battles.
> But late in life, he said his true glory wasn't the wars.
> It was a book. The Civil Code of 1804.
> One clear set of laws for all of France.
> Property, contracts, marriage, all written down in plain words.
> No special privileges by birth.
> Its ideas shaped laws across Europe and far beyond.
> France still uses it today.
> Then his empire fell. The book stayed.

**Fact notes (for us, not read aloud):**
- In exile on Saint Helena, Napoleon is recorded as saying his true glory was not his forty battles but his Civil Code, which would live forever.
- The Civil Code (Code civil des Français) was passed in 1804. It set out one body of civil law for the whole country, covering persons, family, property and contracts.
- It made the end of feudal privileges by birth part of written law.
- It became a model for civil law in many countries in Europe, Latin America and elsewhere, including Louisiana.
- It is still in force in France today, with many changes over time.
- I left out exact article counts and lists of countries on purpose.

## 2. Shot list

The whole video is one continuous camera move. Nothing hard-cuts. Each object slides, fades or is uncovered as the camera travels.

| # | Narration | On screen | Camera |
|---|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | "This is Napoleon's hat." | The bicorne hat, centered, alone on a pale paper background. | Starts close on the hat and slowly drifts back so the whole hat shows. |
| 2 | "Most people remember him for his battles." | A cannon slides in below the hat. A soft blue puff of smoke drifts from the muzzle (added by the animation). | Tilts down slightly to fit the cannon under the hat. |
| 3 | "But late in life, he said his true glory wasn't the wars." | The cannon fades and sinks away. Napoleon's bust rises in its place, under the hat. The words "true glory" appear as an animated label. | Slow push in toward the bust's face. |
| 4 | "It was a book. The Civil Code of 1804." | The bust fades. A closed leather-bound book is revealed below. The animation adds the title "Civil Code" and "1804". | Keeps pushing in until the book fills the frame. |
| 5 | "One clear set of laws for all of France." | The book opens. Its two pages fill the screen, covered in fine unreadable lines that look like printed text. | Moves down into the open pages. |
| 6 | "Property, contracts, marriage, all written down in plain words." | Still on the open pages. The animated labels "Property", "Contracts" and "Marriage" appear one at a time, each with a soft blue underline. | Glides gently down the page, one label at a time. |
| 7 | "No special privileges by birth." | A noble's coronet sits on the page. A soft blue line of light passes over it and it fades away. | Holds, then starts to pull back. |
| 8 | "Its ideas shaped laws across Europe and far beyond." | The book shrinks and settles onto a plain map of Europe. Soft blue lines of light spread outward from France to the edges of the frame. | Pulls back wide to show the whole map. |
| 9 | "France still uses it today." | On the map, France glows soft blue. | Small, slow push toward France. |
| 10 | "Then his empire fell. The book stayed." | The imperial eagle rises over the map, then tips and fades away. The map fades too. Only the closed book is left, and the hat drops gently onto it. | Pulls back to the same framing as shot 1. The hat is in the same place, so the video loops. |

## 4. Style line (word for word)

> Black and white scientific illustration, fine ink lines and soft graphite shading, like an old textbook plate; a single object centered on a plain pale paper background; no text, no labels, no numbers, no color.

*The soft blue accent is added only by the animation, and only for light and movement: the smoke, underlines, spreading lines and glows.*
